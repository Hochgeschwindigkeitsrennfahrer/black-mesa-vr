Black Mesa VR — sprint drop-in
==============================

Sprint with left joystick click (L3). Hold to keep sprinting.

Same install as the usual drop-in: close the game, copy this folder into
Steam\steamapps\common\Black Mesa so d3d9.dll lands in all three:

  Black Mesa\d3d9.dll
  Black Mesa\bin\d3d9.dll
  Black Mesa\bin\thirdparty\dxvk-windows-x86\d3d9.dll

Launch with -oldgameui (and -enabledxvk if the video menu is native D3D9).
